# Business Insights

## Revenue and market

The analysis shows total revenue of around 19.23 million. The UK is the main market, contributing around 16.33 million in revenue. EIRE, Netherlands, Germany and France are the next largest markets, but their revenue is much lower compared with the UK.

## Customer contribution

Registered customers generated around 16.59 million in revenue, while guest customers generated around 2.64 million. Revenue was therefore largely generated by registered customers.

At the customer level, a few customers generated much higher revenue than others. Customer 18102 was the highest at around 598K, followed by 14646 at around 523K.

## Product performance

REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER was the highest-revenue product at around 327K. WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER, JUMBO BAG RED RETROSPOT, PARTY BUNTING and ASSORTED COLOUR BIRD ORNAMENT were also among the top products by revenue.

## Returns

There were 22,889 return transactions after handling records with missing quantities. The total returned quantity was around 1.06 million units. The UK had the highest number of returns, which is also expected given its much larger transaction volume.

## Data quality and non-product records

During the analysis, some records were found that were not actual products. Examples include Bank Charges, Postage, Amazon Fee, Manual, Discount, Commission, Samples and Adjust bad debt. These records were separated from product analysis to avoid affecting product-level results.

The dataset also contained missing Customer IDs, missing descriptions, zero prices and other unusual records. These were checked during the Python analysis before the main business analysis was carried out.

## Monthly performance

Revenue changed noticeably from month to month. November 2011 was the highest-revenue month at around 1.46 million, while December 2011 was around 433K in the final monthly analysis.

## Overall

The main pattern from the analysis is that revenue was heavily concentrated in the UK and among registered customers, while a smaller group of customers and products contributed a large amount of the total revenue. Returns and non-product records were also important to identify because they can change the results if they are included directly in product analysis.
